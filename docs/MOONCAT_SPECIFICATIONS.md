# Moon Cat Launcher — confirmed product requirements
Free with voluntary donations; GitHub, F-Droid and Google Play; GPL-3.0; Android 9+. Spanish and English. Cat sitting on crescent moon; lavender #C4B5FD, midnight blue #15182E, white. No mascot. Catholic biblical quotation in About: John 3:15-17 (owner choice). Daily text on home shows Bible verses supplied by the owner (none yet). No upstream donation links.
Home: text-only default; original icons, monochrome icons with text and icons-only optional; per-screen and per-app overrides. Nested named folders, drag/drop and menu creation, inline default with popup and full-screen alternatives. Multiple configurable pages; initial home and widget page; gestures navigate pages. Native and third-party widgets with privacy warning.
Drawer: list/grid/categories/folders; manual and local automatic categories; local search apps, authorized contacts/files, notes. Configurable gestures, themes (light/dark/AMOLED/custom), animations off option. Local theme editor with preview, save, duplicate, import/export.
Productivity: notes, tasks, calendar, Pomodoro, focus schedules and hiding apps. Privacy: no own network, minimal permissions, local permission inspector, basic/advanced views, biometric protection of settings/folders/launches, hidden apps, encrypted local backups, optional accessibility with risk disclosure. Launcher restrictions are not system-wide app locks. No tracker detector or integrated firewall. Updates through external distribution channels only. Third-party widgets and explicitly opened external apps operate independently.
